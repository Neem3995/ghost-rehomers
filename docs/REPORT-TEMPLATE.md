# REPORT template (one page max)

Name the file REPORT-claude-code.md or REPORT-codex.md in the repo root.

## Role
Your role in one line.

## What I did
Bulleted list of completed work, with file paths. Include commits/branches.

## What works (verified)
What you actually tested and how (command or playtest), with the real result.

## What needs work
Known bugs, gaps, shortcuts, incomplete items, ranked by impact on the Gate 1 exit test.

## Where I got stuck
Problems that cost time or that you couldn't solve; error messages (short); what you tried.

## Untested / assumptions
Anything you did not verify, and any assumption you made.

## Needs from the other agent or from Joseph
Specific requests, decisions or installs.

## Suggested next tasks
Up to 5, in order, each with a clear acceptance test.

## Rough effort
Approx. sessions used and anything that ran past its time box.
